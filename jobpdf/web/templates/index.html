See which remote jobs fit your CV

Upload your CV. You get current remote jobs ranked by how well your skills and experience fit, with the skills you have and the ones you are missing for each.

What the report shows

Each job gets a row. The meter has one segment for every skill the job requires. A filled segment is a skill you have; an outlined one is a skill you are missing.

What happens to your CV

The file is read in memory, turned into a list of skills and experience, and deleted. Nothing from it is stored on our side.

To read the skills, the text of your CV is sent to a language model service (Google Gemini). Do not upload a CV you are not willing to send there.

Uploads are limited per network address. The address is turned into a one-way code for the count and is not stored.
